AnimeInPost

AnimeInPost is an online manga, manhwa, manhua, and novel reading platform.

🌐 Website: https://animeinpost.site/

🌐 Website: https://animeinpost.pages.dev/

Features

- 📚 Browse manga, manhwa, manhua, and novels
- 🔎 Search for series and chapters
- 📖 Read chapters online
- 🔖 Bookmark series
- 🏷️ Browse by genres and types
- 📊 Series ratings and information
- 📝 Alternative titles and author information
- 📑 Chapter listings with newest chapters first
- 📥 Ebook and downloadable reading resources
- 📱 Mobile-friendly reading experience

Series

AnimeInPost provides dedicated pages for individual series containing information such as:

- Title
- Alternative titles
- Author
- Genres
- Rating
- Status
- Type
- Description
- Available chapters
- Bookmarks

Example series pages include:

- The Greatest Estate Developer
- Star Instructor, Master Baek
- Tower of God: Urek Mazino
- Is It Bad That the Main Character's a Roleplayer?
- The Regressed Son of a Duke Is an Assassin

Browse

You can browse the available series through the AnimeInPost series directory:

https://animeinpost.site/series/

Available filters include:

- All
- Ongoing
- On Break
- Hiatus
- Completed

Content can also be filtered by type:

- Manga
- Manhwa
- Manhua
- Novel
